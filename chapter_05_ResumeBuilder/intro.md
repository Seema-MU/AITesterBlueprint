Here's a script you can adapt — I've split it into a **job interview version** (more structured, ~60-75 seconds) and a **shorter networking version** (~30 seconds), both built from your actual background so you're not memorizing lines that don't sound like you.

## For a Job Interview

"I'm Seema M U, a Senior Test Automation Engineer with over 13 years of experience across retail, e-commerce, and construction domains. Right now I'm at Slate Technologies, where I architected our Cypress automation framework using the Page Object Model — that work alone helped the team automate over 200 web and 100 mobile test cases and cut manual testing effort by about 5 days every release cycle.

Before that, I spent eight years at Lowe's India, where I built out hybrid automation frameworks in Robot Framework, Python, and Selenium, and automated 500+ test cases for our Sales & Returns module — work that consistently saved 3 manual testing days per regression cycle. I was recognized as Project Star there for automating high-impact business scenarios.

What I bring beyond the technical toolkit is a track record of building automation strategy from the ground up — not just writing test scripts, but designing the frameworks and CI/CD pipelines that let entire teams move faster with confidence. I also enjoy mentoring — I've coached several newer engineers on automation standards and product knowledge. I'm looking for a role where I can keep doing that: building scalable quality systems and helping teams around me level up."

## For a Networking Event

"Hi, I'm Seema — I've spent the last 13+ years in QA and test automation, mostly recently leading Cypress automation at a construction-tech company here in Bangalore. Before that I was at Lowe's for eight years building out automation frameworks and CI/CD pipelines for a large retail platform. I love the puzzle of designing testing strategy that actually scales with a team, rather than just writing test cases one by one — and I'm always happy to talk shop about automation frameworks if that's your world too."

A few notes on delivery:
- Practice it out loud a couple of times rather than reading it — it'll sound more natural if you're not word-perfect.
- For the interview version, the last line ("I'm looking for a role where...") is a good spot to swap in something specific to the company/role you're interviewing for.
- Trim the mentoring line for the networking version if time's tight — it's a nice-to-have, not essential.
