app.vwo.com is the login dashboard for VWO (Visual Website Optimizer), an all-in-one Digital Experience Optimization and Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) platform managed by Wingify. It is used by businesses to research user behavior, run marketing experiments, and personalize website experiences to increase sales and sign-ups.The main functions of the platform are broken down into specific capabilities:Core CapabilitiesA/B & Multivariate Testing: You can test multiple versions of a website page, button, or layout against each other to see which version converts more visitors into customers.Behavioral Insights: It tracks user movement using heatmaps, clickmaps, and session recordings (video replays of how actual people browse your pages) so you can see where they get stuck or drop off.Funnel & Form Analytics: It isolates exact drop-off points in multi-step processes, such as shopping carts or multi-field registration forms.Personalization: The platform alters website messaging, banners, or product layouts dynamically based on user data, location, or past behavior.Feature Management: Developers can use it to safely roll out new software features, manage feature flags, or run complex server-side code experiments without hurting app performance.On-Page Surveys: You can launch quick target pop-up surveys to collect direct feedback from users right while they are browsing.
