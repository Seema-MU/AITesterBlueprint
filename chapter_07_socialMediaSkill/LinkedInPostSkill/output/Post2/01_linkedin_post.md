## Full Post

Your Cypress suite isn't a framework. It's a pile of scripts that happen to pass.

The hard part was never writing the tests. I learned that the second time I built a Cypress framework from an empty repo.

Anyone can write a passing test.

The hard part is everything that shows up in month six:

🔹 Can a new engineer read this and know where to add a test?
🔹 When something fails at 2am, can you tell WHY in under five minutes?
🔹 Does a UI change break one file or forty?
🔹 Is your test data reusable, or copy-pasted into every spec?
🔹 Does this actually run in CI, or only on your machine?

A test script proves a feature worked once.
A framework proves your team can keep proving it.

That's the difference between 500 automated test cases and 500 automated test cases anyone trusts.

If your suite went red tomorrow, would your team investigate — or just rerun it and hope?
