## Full Post

AI can generate your test cases. It can't sign off on your quality report. That's still on you.

Everyone's arguing whether manual testing is dying or automation is overhyped. Wrong argument.

The real shift happening right now: AI can write test cases, generate SDET documents, suggest scenarios — fast, plausible, confident. What it can't do is know which failure would actually hurt the business, or catch the thing that "looks fine" but isn't, because it doesn't understand your product the way a user does.

That's not a job for automation. It's not even really a "manual vs. automated" question anymore. It's a credibility question — someone has to be able to look at what AI produced and actually vouch for it, not just forward it along because it sounded right.

Learn the automation. Learn the AI tools. Use both. But don't confuse "I ran the prompt" with "I understand what I just tested."

The tester who can do both — use the tools AND catch what they miss — isn't being replaced. They're becoming the only one clients can actually trust.

If AI generated your last test plan, could you defend every line of it in a review? Or did you just... trust it?
